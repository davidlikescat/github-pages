Hacker News "Show HN"

제목: Show HN: JustBlurt – Voice notes that route themselves into the right Notion database

Hi HN, I'm Joonmo, an engineering manager at a Korean telco. I built JustBlurt as a nights-and-weekends project to solve a problem I had: I live in Notion, but capturing thoughts on mobile was so slow I'd forget them. Existing voice-to-text apps (AudioPen, Voicenotes, etc.) all stop at "beautiful text." You still have to copy-paste into the right Notion database. JustBlurt does that part — it classifies intent (idea vs task vs weekly inbox) and routes the transcript automatically. Tech stack: React Native (Expo), Whisper for transcription, Claude for intent classification, Notion API for routing. Intent accuracy is at 89% after 8 weeks of prompt iteration (started at 67%). Things I'd love HN feedback on: 1. The intent classification approach — few-shot vs fine-tune for this kind of small-set routing? 2. The pricing model (currently planning $5/mo or $40/yr) 3. Whether to add team features now or wait for individual traction Beta: [link] Build log on X: [link] Thanks for reading.
